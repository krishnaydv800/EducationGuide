BEFORE ADMISSION

7 things to verify

  1. Course eligibility
  2. Recognition / accreditation
  3. Total cost, not only tuition
  4. Scholarship availability
  5. Faculty and facilities
  6. Internship / placement information
  7. Official admission dates

⚠️ Verify current information

College fees, admission dates, eligibility, counselling rules and exam requirements can change. Always verify the latest information from the official institution or exam website before applying or paying.
